Cerberus gun model
==================

Files:
  cerberus.obj         - mesh (positions, normals, UVs; single material)
  cerberus_albedo.png  - 2048x2048 albedo texture (converted from A.dds via ffmpeg)

Author : Andrew Maximov (artisaverb.info)
Source : "Cerberus" free PBR test asset, widely redistributed for graphics
         demos. OBJ + DDS copies obtained from the open-source renderer repo
         https://github.com/jian-ru/laugh_engine (models/cerberus.obj,
         textures/Cerberus/A.dds).
